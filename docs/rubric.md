# The Hargrove Family Scholarship

## About the Award

**Amount:** $20,000 a year for four years, renewable
**Available:** 3
**Applies To:** graduating high school seniors in our service area

Here's what the donor asked for, in her words: fund the students this actually changes things for. She wasn't looking for the most decorated kid in the pile.

## How We Score

Score each applicant 0–10 on all five criteria. The weighted total is their score.

| Criterion                 | Weight | What we mean                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------- | ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Academic readiness        | 30%    | Can this student handle a four-year program? Look at where they're headed, not just where they are — and look at it in context. What classes did their school actually offer? What else were they carrying while they took them? |
| Financial need            | 25%    | How much does this award decide the outcome? We're asking whether it's the difference, not whether it helps.                                                                                                                     |
| Persistence               | 20%    | Did they keep going through something hard? The size of the obstacle isn't the point. Plenty of people quit over less.                                                                                                           |
| Contribution to community | 15%    | Service, leadership, taking care of people. Weigh it against what was realistically available to them — a kid working 20 hours a week can't rack up volunteer hours, and hours were never a great measure anyway.                |
| Clarity of purpose        | 10%    | Do they have a specific, believable idea of what they want to do and why?                                                                                                                                                        |

If you land on a tie right at the cutoff, that's the committee's role to sort out. Don't break it arbitrarily.

## What We Don't Score

Athletics. How prestigious their high school is. How prestigious the school they're applying to is. How polished the writing is — as distinct from what they're actually saying.

## One Note on the Essays

These are seventeen-year-olds. We've asked our reviewers not to mistake good prose for good thinking. Our strongest applicants are not reliably our strongest writers, and we've gotten that wrong before.
