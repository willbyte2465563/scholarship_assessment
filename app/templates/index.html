How this works

  1. Held applicants: decide whether each should be scored in the next batch run or excluded.
  2. Shortlist: pick 2 to 4 to compare side by side. Override a criterion score there if you disagree, with a reason.
  3. Final 3: record exactly three from the shortlist, with a reason. Names are shown only after this.

You see applicant IDs and the anonymized application only. Every action is written to the audit log with your name. There's no login; in production this would be SSO.
